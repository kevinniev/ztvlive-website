## VIDEO 1: Game Demo Walkthrough (60-90 seconds)
### Style: Clean, Professional, Screen Recording with Voiceover

**SCENE 1: Opening (0-5 sec)**
- Show ZTVLIVE logo animation
- Text: "24/7 LIVE Interactive Game Show"

**SCENE 2: Introduction (5-15 sec)**
- Show the /play page loading on mobile
- VOICEOVER: "Welcome to ZTVLIVE - the world's first 24/7 interactive game show where YOU compete for real prizes, anytime, anywhere."

**SCENE 3: Live Question (15-30 sec)**
- Screen capture of question appearing: "Cash or card - how do YOU pay?"
- Show the timer counting down
- Show "70 players • 59 answers" stats updating live
- VOICEOVER: "Every question syncs live across thousands of players worldwide. You have 30 seconds to type your answer."

**SCENE 4: Answering (30-40 sec)**
- Show typing an answer
- Show submit button being tapped
- Show the "Submitted!" confirmation
- VOICEOVER: "Just type what you think most people would say. Match the top answers to earn points!"

**SCENE 5: Results (40-55 sec)**
- Show top answers revealing
- Show leaderboard updating
- Show score increasing
- VOICEOVER: "When time's up, see how you ranked against everyone else. Top players win mystery prizes - real gift cards, delivered instantly!"

**SCENE 6: TV Experience (55-70 sec)**
- Show the Roku/TV broadcast view
- Show QR code being scanned
- VOICEOVER: "Watch on your big screen TV via Roku or Fire TV. Scan the QR code to play along from your phone!"

**SCENE 7: Call to Action (70-90 sec)**
- Show multiple languages available
- Show share button
- Text overlay: "PLAY NOW - ztvlivestream.com/play"
- VOICEOVER: "Available in 20 languages. No signup required. Just tap and play. Join the fun at ztvlivestream.com/play"

## SCREEN RECORDING INSTRUCTIONS:

### Mobile Recording (for social media vertical):
1. Use iPhone/Android screen recorder
2. Record at 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical)
3. Go to: https://ztvlivestream.com/play
4. Record a full 3-4 minute game cycle
5. Edit down to 60-90 seconds

### TV Recording (for website/YouTube):
1. Use OBS or screen capture
2. Record at 1920x1080 (16:9)
3. Go to: https://ztvlivestream.com/roku-tv
4. Capture the full TV broadcast experience
5. Show QR code scanning with phone

---

## AUDIO RECOMMENDATIONS:

### Background Music:
- Use the embedded game audio (35% volume) OR
- Royalty-free upbeat music (100-120 BPM)
- Clean, modern, game show vibe

### Voiceover Tips:
- Clear, friendly, enthusiastic
- Professional but not corporate
- Emphasize "LIVE", "FREE", "WIN"

## EXPORT SETTINGS:

### For Social Media (TikTok/Reels/Shorts):
- 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical)
- 30fps or 60fps
- H.264 codec
- Bitrate: 10-15 Mbps

### For Website/YouTube:
- 1920x1080 (16:9 horizontal)
- 30fps or 60fps
- H.264 codec
- Bitrate: 15-20 Mbps
