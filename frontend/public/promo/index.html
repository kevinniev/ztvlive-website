🎬 How to Record the Live Game

For TV Broadcast (Horizontal)

  1. Click "Open Fullscreen" on TV Broadcast above
  2. Use OBS or screen recorder at 1920x1080
  3. Record 2-3 full question cycles (3-4 minutes)
  4. Edit down to 60-90 seconds
  5. The AI players will demonstrate the full experience!

For Mobile (Vertical - Social Media)

  1. Open /play on your phone
  2. Use phone's screen recorder
  3. Record at 1080x1920 (9:16)
  4. Capture answering a question + results
  5. Add text overlays in your video editor
